The ensemble

Six models each predict every game. Their weights are fit on out-of-sample predictions only, and a model gets zero weight when the others already cover what it knows.

Brier score by season

Lower is better. Every season is predicted by a model trained only on earlier seasons.

Calibration

When the model says 70%, the home team should win about 70% of the time.

How it works

Data comes from nflverse play-by-play (2006 to now). Each game gets pregame-only features: Elo ratings, decayed offensive and defensive EPA per play and success rate, a quarterback rating built from the starter's dropback EPA, rest, and a few game flags. Six models (logistic regression, LightGBM, a point-margin regression, extra trees, a small neural net, and Elo) are combined in a stacked ensemble. A scheduled job refreshes data and predictions every week and snapshots Kalshi prices several times a day, logging them so the model can be graded against the exact prices that were available.

Kalshi edge is the model's chance minus the ask price minus Kalshi's taker fee. Positive means the model thinks that contract is cheap. Paper results only.
